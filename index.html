What SyncWave does

It turns phones, laptops and speakers into one synchronized sound system. Every device plays the same queue at the same moment, and each one can play its own channel: left, right, center, subwoofer or surrounds.

🏠

1. Host a room

On the device that will control the music, tap Host a room and give it a name. As the host you control play, pause, skipping, the queue and every speaker. The host link is saved on this device, so you can come back to it.

📲

2. Invite devices

In the room, tap Share to show a QR code, the 6-character room code and the link. On every other device, scan the QR code or tap Join a room and type the code.

👆

3. Join with sound

Each device taps Join with sound once (browsers need a tap before they play audio). Keep the page open: phones may pause audio when the screen locks.

🎛️

4. Set up the speakers

Open Speakers to see the room map. Pick a layout (2.0, 2.1, 3.1, 5.1 or 7.1) and assign each device a role, or use auto-assign. Calibrate uses the host's microphone to line every speaker up, including Bluetooth delay.

🎵

5. Add music

Upload files, paste links, or search podcasts, Archive.org, Audius, Jamendo and YouTube. Listeners can preview a song and send a request; only the host sees it and approves or rejects it.

💾

6. Pick up later

The queue, play history, speakers and playlists are saved. Reopen a room from Join a room → Your rooms, or reload the page: you go straight back in.
