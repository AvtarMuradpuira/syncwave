SyncWave

Every device. One sound.

Turn phones, laptops and speakers into one synchronized system. Assign each one a channel (left, right, center, subwoofer, surrounds) and play from a shared queue that everyone can add to.

Start a session

You'll be the host: you control playback, the queue and speaker roles.

Join a session

Enter the 6-character code from the host's screen, or open their link.

Your sessions

Sample-scheduled sync

Devices share a clock over WebSocket and start every track on the same audio-clock instant, then hold it there with tiny rate corrections.

Surround from any device

Stereo, mono, L/R, center, LFE and surrounds for 2.1, 5.1 and 7.1. Multichannel files keep their channels; stereo files are upmixed.

Shared queue

Upload files, paste links, browse podcasts, Archive.org and Jamendo, or add YouTube videos. Listeners suggest and vote.

Sessions that last

Queue, history, devices and playlists are saved, so you can pick up where you left off.
